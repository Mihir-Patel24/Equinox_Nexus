# Israel — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System — 2024 Rates
Israel has a progressive income tax system:
- Up to ILS 7,010/month: 10%
- ILS 7,011–10,060/month: 14%
- ILS 10,061–16,150/month: 20%
- ILS 16,151–21,400/month: 31%
- ILS 21,401–46,640/month: 35%
- ILS 46,641–60,130/month: 47%
- Above ILS 60,130/month: 50%

**Point credits (נקודות זיכוי)**: Each resident gets 2.25 points × ILS 2,904/year = tax credit
Additional points for: children, new immigrants, women, etc.

### National Insurance (Bituach Leumi) & Health Tax
- National Insurance: approximately 3.5–7% (employee share, based on income)
- Health tax: 3.1%–5%
Combined: approximately 8.5–12%

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income ILS 400,000/year: approximately 40–46% combined

## New Immigrant (Oleh) Tax Benefits
One of the most generous immigrant tax regimes globally:
- **10-year exemption** on foreign-sourced income and assets (tax-free period)
- Applies to: salary from foreign employers, dividends, interest, capital gains on foreign assets
- All reporting on foreign assets is waived for 10 years
- Available to: Jewish immigrants returning to Israel (Aliyah) and their spouses

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Israel has DTAs with 60+ countries.

### DTA with India
Israel-India DTA (not in force as of 2024 — India and Israel do not have a comprehensive DTA)
Israeli residents earning Indian income must rely on domestic relief provisions.

### DTA with USA
Israel-US DTA (1994): US citizens in Israel still owe US taxes; FEIE and Foreign Tax Credit apply.

## Work Visas

### B-1 Work Visa
- For specialized skilled workers
- Employer must demonstrate no available Israeli worker
- Duration: 1 year, renewable (long-term workers may apply for B-1 multi-year)

### Innovation Authority Visas
For tech workers at companies receiving Israeli Innovation Authority grants.

## VAT (מע"מ, Maam)
Standard rate: 18% (increased from 17% in January 2024)
Zero-rated: exports, international transport, certain fresh produce

## Cost-of-Living Context
Israel is one of the world's most expensive countries:
- Tel Aviv 1-bedroom central: ILS 6,500–10,000/month (~$1,800–$2,800 USD)
- Jerusalem: 10–15% cheaper than Tel Aviv
- Monthly Rav-Kav transport pass (Tel Aviv): approximately ILS 250/month
- Israel's high-tech sector is world-class (Silicon Wadi)
- Very high housing costs; rent burden is severe in Tel Aviv
