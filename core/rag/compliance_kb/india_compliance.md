# India — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System — FY 2024-25

### New Tax Regime (Default from FY 2024-25)
- Up to INR 3,00,000: 0%
- INR 3,00,001–7,00,000: 5%
- INR 7,00,001–10,00,000: 10%
- INR 10,00,001–12,00,000: 15%
- INR 12,00,001–15,00,000: 20%
- Above INR 15,00,000: 30%

**Standard deduction (New regime)**: INR 75,000/year
**Tax rebate u/s 87A**: Up to INR 25,000 (for income up to INR 7 lakh — effectively 0 tax)

### Old Tax Regime (Optional)
- Standard deductions including 80C (INR 1.5L), HRA, LTA, 80D (health insurance)
- More beneficial for those with significant deductions

### Surcharges
- Income INR 50L–1Cr: 10% surcharge on tax
- INR 1Cr–2Cr: 15% surcharge
- INR 2Cr–5Cr: 25% surcharge
- Above INR 5Cr: 37% surcharge (reduced to 25% for new regime from FY23-24)

### Effective Marginal Rate (High Earners)
Income INR 5Cr+: approximately 42.7% (30% + surcharge + cess)

### Provident Fund (EPF)
- Employee: 12% of basic salary
- Employer: 12% (3.67% to EPF, 8.33% to EPS)
Both contributions tax-deductible under old regime

## Overseas Citizens of India (OCI)
OCI cardholders can work in India without a work permit. OCI status is granted to foreign nationals of Indian origin. They are treated as NRIs for tax purposes when not resident in India.

## Tax Residency Rules
An individual is a Resident if:
- Present in India ≥182 days in FY, OR
- Present in India ≥60 days in FY AND ≥365 days in preceding 4 years
- NRI (Non-Resident Indian): taxed only on Indian-sourced income

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
India has DTAs with 95+ countries.

### Common DTA Benefits for Indian Expats
- Foreign Tax Credit: Indian residents can claim credit for tax paid abroad
- DTAA protections prevent double taxation of salary earned abroad if treaty conditions are met

## GST (Goods and Services Tax)
- Standard rate: 18% (most services and manufactured goods)
- 28% slab: luxury goods, sin goods
- 12% slab: processed foods, computers
- 5% slab: essential items
- 0%: basic food, healthcare, education

## Investing as NRI
NRIs can invest via:
- NRE (Non-Resident External) account: tax-free in India; freely repatriable
- NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) account: income from India; taxable
- Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) route for stock market
- Real estate: yes, except agricultural land
