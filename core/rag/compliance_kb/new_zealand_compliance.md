# New Zealand — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System — 2024/25 Rates (Updated October 2023)
New Zealand restructured its top tax brackets:
- Up to NZD 14,000: 10.5%
- NZD 14,001–48,000: 17.5%
- NZD 48,001–70,000: 30%
- NZD 70,001–180,000: 33%
- Above NZD 180,000: 39%

**No capital gains tax** (with exceptions for property speculation rules)
**No inheritance/estate tax**

### ACC (Accident Compensation Corporation) Levy
- Employee: approximately 1.39% of taxable income (varies annually)
- Provides cover for personal injuries (unique no-fault system)

### KiwiSaver
New Zealand's voluntary (but default opt-in) retirement savings:
- Employee minimum: 3% of gross salary
- Employer minimum: 3% of gross salary
- Additional government contribution: up to NZD 521/year

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income NZD 120,000/year: approximately 32–35%

## Transitional Tax Relief for New Residents
For the **first 4 years of tax residency** in NZ:
- Foreign-sourced income is generally exempt from NZ tax
- Investment income from offshore is not taxed during this period
- Very beneficial for high-net-worth individuals relocating to NZ

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
New Zealand has DTAs with 40+ countries.

### DTA with India
New Zealand-India DTA (1986):
- Employment income: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 15%
- Interest: WHT 10%
- Royalties: WHT 10%

## Work Visas

### Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV)
Primary work visa system since 2022:
- Employer must be accredited by Immigration NZ
- Job must meet median wage (NZD 29.66/hour; NZD 29.66 in 2024)
- Tied to employer; can switch to new accredited employer

### Skilled Migrant Category Resident Visa (SMC)
Points-based permanent residency:
- Points: qualifications, work experience, job offer, partner qualifications, age

### Green List Occupations (Fast-Track Residency)
Two tiers:
- Tier 1 (Straight to Residence): e.g., specialist doctors, surgeons, some tech roles
- Tier 2 (Work-to-Residence): e.g., nurses, engineers, ICT professionals

## GST (Goods and Services Tax)
Standard rate: 15% (flat; one of simplest GST systems globally)
No reduced rates; all goods and services charged same rate (with exemptions for financial services, residential rents)

## Cost-of-Living Context
- Auckland 1-bedroom central: NZD 2,200–3,200/month (~$1,300–$1,900 USD)
- Wellington: similar to Auckland
- Christchurch: 20–30% cheaper than Auckland
- Monthly Hop card transport (Auckland): NZD 200/month approximately
- New Zealand is relatively isolated but has extremely high quality of life and natural beauty
