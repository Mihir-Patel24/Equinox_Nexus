### Effective Tax Burden

## Non-Habitual Resident (NHR) Regime
**One of the most attractive expat tax regimes in Europe.**
- 10-year tax benefit for new residents
- **20% flat rate** on Portuguese-sourced qualifying income (vs up to 48% standard)
- **0% tax** on most foreign-sourced income (dividends, interest, rental income, pensions)
- Applies to: high-value-added activities (architects, engineers, IT professionals, doctors, investors)
- Eligibility: must not have been a Portuguese tax resident in the previous 5 years

**NHR 2.0 (IFICI Regime) — from January 2024:**
The original NHR was replaced by the IFICI regime, still offering significant tax advantages for highly qualified professionals and scientific researchers.

## Work Visas

### D3 Visa (Highly Qualified Professional)
- For highly qualified specialists and senior management
- Requires employment contract or job offer
- Valid 1 year, renewable for 2+2 years, then permanent residency

### D8 Visa (Digital Nomad / Remote Worker)
- For remote workers earning ≥4× Portuguese minimum wage (≈€3,040/month as of 2024)
- 1-year initial period, renewable
- One of the most popular digital nomad visas globally

### Golden Visa (ARI)
- Investment residency program
- As of 2024, property investment route closed; still available via:
  - Investment funds (≥€500,000)
  - Job creation (≥10 jobs)
  - Capital transfer (≥€1.5M)
- Leads to citizenship in 5 years

## VAT (IVA)
- Standard rate: 23% (Mainland)
- Intermediate rate: 13% (wine, oil, some food)
- Reduced rate: 6% (basic food, books, medicine)
