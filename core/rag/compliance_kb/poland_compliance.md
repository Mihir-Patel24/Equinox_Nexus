# Poland — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System — 2024 Rates
Poland revised its tax system significantly in 2022:
- Up to PLN 120,000: 12%
- Above PLN 120,000: 32%
- Solidary levy: 4% on income above PLN 1,000,000

**Tax-free threshold**: PLN 30,000/year (zero income tax)

### Social Security Contributions (Employee) — ZUS
- Pension (emerytalne): 9.76%
- Disability (rentowe): 1.5%
- Sickness insurance (chorobowe): 2.45%
- Health insurance (NFZ): 9% (income tax-deductible portion: 7.75%)
Total employee: approximately 22.71%

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income PLN 120,000/year: approximately 32–38% combined

## IP Box (Innovation Box)
5% preferential tax rate on income from qualifying intellectual property rights.
Popular for IT professionals and software developers working on their own IP.

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Poland has DTAs with 90+ countries.

### DTA with India
Poland-India DTA (1990):
- Employment income: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 15%
- Interest: WHT 15%
- Royalties: WHT 22.5%

## Work Visas

### EU Blue Card
Standard EU pathway for highly skilled workers.

### Temporary Residence Permit (TRP) for Work
- Work permit (zezwolenie na pracę) + visa D or TRP
- Simplified procedure for employers registering workers from select countries
- Ukrainian nationals have simplified access post-2022

### Poland Business Harbor
Program for IT professionals from select countries (Ukraine, Russia, Belarus, Georgia, Armenia, Moldova):
- Fast-tracked residency for tech workers
- Employer sponsor required

## VAT (PTU)
- Standard rate: 23%
- Reduced rate: 8% (food, medicine, medical equipment)
- Reduced rate: 5% (basic food, books)
- Zero rate: 0% (exports)

## Cost-of-Living Context
Poland offers exceptional value for money:
- Warsaw 1-bedroom central: PLN 3,500–5,500/month (~€800–€1,300)
- Krakow, Wroclaw, Gdansk: 15–30% cheaper than Warsaw
- Monthly ZTM transport pass (Warsaw): PLN 110/month (~€26)
- Strong IT and BPO sector; major office for Google, Allegro, CD Projekt, etc.
- Poland is joining Schengen air border fully (Schengen member since 2007)
