# South Korea — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System — 2024 Rates
South Korea has a progressive income tax system:
- Up to KRW 14,000,000: 6%
- KRW 14M–50M: 15%
- KRW 50M–88M: 24%
- KRW 88M–150M: 35%
- KRW 150M–300M: 38%
- KRW 300M–500M: 40%
- KRW 500M–1,000M: 42%
- Above KRW 1,000M: 45%

**Local Income Tax**: Additional 10% of the income tax amount (effectively adds ~0.6–4.5% to marginal rate)

### Social Insurance Contributions (Employee)
- National Pension: 4.5%
- National Health Insurance: 3.545%
- Employment Insurance: 0.9%
- Long-term Care Insurance: 0.46%
Total employee: approximately 9.4%

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income KRW 80M/year: approximately 28–32% combined

## Flat Tax Rate for Foreigners
Foreigners working in Korea can elect a **flat 19% income tax rate** (no deductions) instead of the standard progressive rates. This is advantageous for high earners. The election must be made for each tax year.

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
South Korea has DTAs with 100+ countries.

### DTA with India
Korea-India DTA (1985):
- Employment income: taxed where work is performed
- Dividends: WHT 15%
- Interest: WHT 10%
- Royalties: WHT 10%

## Work Visas

### E-7 Visa (Specially Designated Activities)
- For skilled professionals in specific occupational categories
- Requires a job offer from a Korean employer
- Employer must receive designation from Ministry of Justice

### F-2 Visa (거주, Residence)
- Long-term residency for highly skilled workers after meeting point-based requirements
- Leads to F-5 (Permanent Residency) after 5+ years

### D-8 Visa (Corporate Investment)
- For executives/employees of foreign-invested companies

### Start-Up Visa
- For entrepreneurs establishing companies in Korea's technology hubs

## National Health Insurance
All residents (including foreigners staying 6+ months) must enroll in the National Health Insurance Service (NHIS). Employee contributions: 3.545% of salary.

## VAT (부가가치세, Bugagachise)
Standard rate: 10%
Zero-rated: exports, international transport

## Cost-of-Living Context
- Seoul 1-bedroom central: KRW 1,200,000–2,000,000/month (~$900–$1,500 USD)
- Jeonse system (Korean deposit): large deposit (40–80% of property value) in exchange for zero rent — common for long-term residents
- Excellent public transport: monthly pass ~KRW 65,000 (~$50)
- Medical costs very low compared to US/UK due to universal health insurance
