# Italy — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax (IRPEF) — 2024 Rates
- Up to €28,000: 23%
- €28,001–50,000: 35%
- Above €50,000: 43%

**Regional Tax (Addizionale Regionale)**: 1.23%–3.33% (varies by region)
**Municipal Tax (Addizionale Comunale)**: 0%–0.9% (varies by municipality)

### Social Security (Employee)
- INPS (pension + social security): approximately 9.19% (employee portion)
- Employer: approximately 29–33%

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income €60,000/year: approximately 43–48% combined

## Expat Tax Regime — Impatriate Regime (Regime degli Impatriati)
**Significant reform in 2024 (for those moving to Italy from 2024):**
- **50% of income** exempt from IRPEF for 5 years (60% if minimum 2 children <18 in Italy)
- Was previously 70% exemption for new regime arrivals — reduced but still valuable
- Eligibility: not been tax resident in Italy for 3 prior years (was 2 prior years)
- Must commit to stay 4 years (was 2 years)
- Limited to income up to €600,000

**Flat Tax for High Earners (New Residents):**
- Optional €200,000 flat tax on all foreign-sourced income per year
- For wealthy individuals relocating to Italy
- Duration: up to 15 years

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Italy has DTAs with 100+ countries.

### DTA with India
Italy-India DTA (1995):
- Employment income: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 15%
- Interest: WHT 10% (15% if bank)
- Royalties: WHT 20% (standard; reduced under treaty)

## Work Visas

### EU Blue Card
Standard pathway for highly skilled non-EU workers.

### Talent Visa (Visto Talento)
For highly qualified professionals:
- Investors (€500,000+ in Italian companies)
- Startup founders
- Researchers and professors
- Highly qualified workers (managerial or specialized roles)

### Flows Decree (Decreto Flussi)
Annual quota-based system for non-EU workers in sectors with labor shortages.

## VAT (IVA)
- Standard rate: 22%
- Reduced rate: 10% (food, hotel, certain services)
- Reduced rate: 5% (some social services)
- Super-reduced: 4% (essential food, books, medicine)

## Cost-of-Living Context
- Milan 1-bedroom central: €1,500–€2,500/month
- Rome: €1,200–€2,200/month
- Florence: €1,000–€1,800/month
- Turin, Bologna: 20–30% cheaper than Milan
- Southern Italy (Naples, Palermo): very affordable (€500–€1,000/month 1-bedroom)
- Monthly ATM pass (Milan): €39/month
