# Hong Kong SAR — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System — 2024/25 (Salaries Tax)
Hong Kong has one of the world's simplest and lowest income tax systems:

### Progressive Rates (Net Chargeable Income)
- Up to HKD 50,000: 2%
- HKD 50,001–100,000: 6%
- HKD 100,001–150,000: 10%
- HKD 150,001–200,000: 14%
- Above HKD 200,000: 17%

**Standard Rate**: 16% flat on net income (choose whichever is lower)
**Maximum Effective Rate**: approximately 15–16% (one of the world's lowest)

No capital gains tax. No dividends tax. No VAT/GST. No inheritance tax. No estate duty.

### Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF)
Mandatory retirement savings for all employees aged 18–64:
- Employee: 5% of relevant income (capped at HKD 1,500/month)
- Employer: 5% of relevant income (capped at HKD 1,500/month)
Lower-income workers may be exempt.

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income HKD 600,000/year: approximately 10–13% combined

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Hong Kong has DTAs/CDTAs with 45 territories (expanding).

### CDTA with India
Hong Kong-India CDTA (2018):
- Employment income: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 5% (if beneficial owner holds ≥10%)
- Interest: WHT 10%
- Royalties: WHT 10%

## Work Visas

### Employment Visa
For professionals working for Hong Kong employers:
- No quota system or labour market test
- Merit-based; employer must demonstrate genuine need for non-local

### Top Talent Pass Scheme (TTPS) — 2022
For highly skilled individuals from anywhere globally:
- Tier A: Annual income HKD 2.5M+ in recent years (no job offer needed)
- Tier B: Graduated from top 100 universities with ≥3 years experience
- Tier C: Graduated from top 100 universities in last 5 years (annual quota applies)
- 2-year initial stay, extendable

### Quality Migrant Admission Scheme (QMAS)
Points-based system for highly skilled individuals; flexible quota; no prior job offer required.

## Sales Tax
**No VAT or sales tax in Hong Kong** (unique among major global financial centers)

## Cost-of-Living Context
Hong Kong has the world's most expensive private housing market:
- 1-bedroom apartment in Central/Wan Chai: HKD 20,000–35,000/month (~$2,600–$4,500 USD)
- Kowloon/New Territories: 25–40% cheaper
- Monthly MTR pass: approximately HKD 600–900/month
- Public housing (HOS): only for permanent residents; extremely long queues
- Food costs are moderate; hawker centres and local eateries are affordable
- Excellent public transit; cars are generally unnecessary
