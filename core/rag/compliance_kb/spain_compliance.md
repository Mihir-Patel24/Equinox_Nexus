# Spain — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax (IRPF) — 2024 Rates
Spain has both state and regional income taxes. Total combined rates:
- Up to €12,450: 19%
- €12,450–20,200: 24%
- €20,200–35,200: 30%
- €35,200–60,000: 37%
- €60,000–300,000: 45%
- Above €300,000: 47%

Regional variations: Catalonia (Barcelona) applies additional regional taxes; total top rate can reach 50%+.
Basque Country has its own tax system (lower effective rates than Spain generally).

### Social Security Contributions (Employee) — 2024

- Professional training: 0.1%

Total employee: approximately 6.55%

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income €70,000/year: approximately 38–44% combined

## Beckham Law (Ley Beckham / Régimen de Impatriados)
Named after David Beckham who famously used it:
- **15% flat tax rate** on Spanish-sourced income (up to €600,000)
- Foreign income: only taxable if certain conditions met
- Duration: 6 years (year of arrival + 5 subsequent years)
- Eligibility: moved to Spain for work, not resident in Spain in previous 5 years
- Applies to: employees, directors, digital nomads (from 2023), remote workers

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Spain has DTAs with 100+ countries.

### DTA with India
Spain-India DTA (1993):
- Employment income: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 15%
- Interest: WHT 15%
- Royalties: WHT 10–20%

## Work Visas

### EU Blue Card
Standard EU pathway for highly skilled workers.

### Digital Nomad Visa (Nómada Digital) — 2023
Spain launched one of Europe's most comprehensive digital nomad visas:
- For remote workers earning from foreign companies/clients
- Minimum income: €2,646/month (2024; 200% of Spanish minimum wage)
- Initial 1-year permit, renewable for 2+2 years
- Beckham Law (15% tax) applies to eligible digital nomads

### Non-Lucrative Visa (NLV)
For those with sufficient passive income:
- Minimum income: €27,792/year (2024; for individual)
- Cannot work for Spanish employers

## VAT (IVA)
- Standard rate: 21%
- Reduced rate: 10% (food at restaurants, hotels, transport)
- Super-reduced: 4% (basic food, books, medicine)

## Cost-of-Living Context
- Madrid 1-bedroom central: €1,400–€2,200/month (2024)
- Barcelona: similar to Madrid or slightly higher
- Valencia, Seville, Malaga: 25–40% cheaper than Madrid/Barcelona
- Monthly metro pass: Madrid €54.60/month; Barcelona €80/month
- Spain has excellent quality of life, food, and climate relative to cost
