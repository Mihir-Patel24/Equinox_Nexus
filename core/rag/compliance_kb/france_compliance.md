# France — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System
France uses a progressive income tax system with a household-based calculation ("quotient familial").

### 2024 Income Tax Brackets (Single Individual)
- Up to €11,294: 0%
- €11,295–€28,797: 11%
- €28,798–€82,341: 30%
- €82,342–€177,106: 41%
- Above €177,106: 45%

### Social Contributions (Cotisations Sociales)
Employees pay substantial social contributions on top of income tax:
- Health insurance (maladie): 0.75% up to ceiling + 2.5% above
- Unemployment (chômage): 2.4%
- Retirement (retraite): ~6.9% base + 0.4% supplementary
- CSG/CRDS: 9.7% on gross
Total employee social contributions: approximately 22–28% of gross salary

### Effective Total Tax Burden
- Income €60,000/year: approximately 42–48% all-in (income tax + social contributions)
- Income €100,000/year: approximately 50–55% all-in
France has one of the highest total tax burdens in the OECD.

## Expat Tax Regimes
### Impatriate Regime (Régime des Impatriés)
For employees recruited abroad and posted to France by a foreign company:
- 50% exemption on foreign-sourced income
- 30% of salary exempted from income tax (impatriate bonus)
- Duration: up to 8 years
- Condition: must not have been French tax resident in 5 previous years

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
France has DTAs with over 125 countries.

### DTA with India
The France-India DTA (1994) prevents double taxation on:
- Salary: taxed in country of activity
- Dividends: WHT 10%
- Interest: WHT 10%
- Royalties: WHT 10%

### DTA with USA
The France-US DTA (1994, updated 2009) is comprehensive. US citizens in France still owe US taxes but receive Foreign Tax Credit.

## Work Visa Categories

### EU Blue Card
For highly skilled non-EU workers:
- Minimum salary: 1.5× average gross annual salary (≈€53,500 in 2024)
- Valid 4 years, renewable
- Leads to permanent residency after 5 years

### Talent Passport (Passeport Talent)
Covers 10 categories including:
- Employees of innovative companies
- Highly qualified employees (salary ≥ 2× minimum wage)
- Researchers and scientists
- Artists of international renown

### Entrepreneur Visa / French Tech Visa
For startup founders with a project supported by a French public entity or accredited incubator.

## Social Security
- France has one of the most comprehensive social security systems globally
- Healthcare largely covered by Sécurité Sociale (70% reimbursement, top-up insurance covers remainder)
- Mandatory health insurance: yes
- Unemployment coverage: yes (if previously contributed)

## VAT (TVA)
- Standard rate: 20%
- Reduced rate: 10% (restaurants, hotels, some entertainment)
- Reduced rate: 5.5% (food, books, disability equipment)
- Super-reduced: 2.1% (certain medicines)

## Banking
Major banks: BNP Paribas, Société Générale, Crédit Agricole, LCL.
Opening a bank account requires proof of address (EDF bill, lease agreement) and identity (passport + visa).
Online banks (N26, Revolut) are popular for initial banking.

## Cost-of-Living Context
Paris is among the most expensive cities in Europe:
- 1-bedroom apartment central Paris: €1,800–€2,800/month (2024)
- Lyon, Marseille, Toulouse: 40–50% cheaper than Paris
- Monthly transport pass (Navigo, Paris): €86.40/month
