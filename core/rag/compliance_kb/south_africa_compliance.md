# South Africa — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax — 2024/25 Rates
South Africa has a progressive income tax system:
- Up to ZAR 237,100: 18%
- ZAR 237,101–370,500: 26%
- ZAR 370,501–512,800: 31%
- ZAR 512,801–673,000: 36%
- ZAR 673,001–857,900: 39%
- ZAR 857,901–1,817,000: 41%
- Above ZAR 1,817,001: 45%

**Primary Rebate**: ZAR 17,235/year (reduces tax owed)
**Effective 0% threshold** (after rebate): approximately ZAR 95,750/year

### Unemployment Insurance Fund (UIF)
- Employee: 1% of gross salary (capped at ZAR 177.12/month)
- Employer: 1% (matching)

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income ZAR 500,000/year: approximately 26–30%

## Foreign Employment Exemption (Section 10(1)(o)(ii))
From 2020, South African tax residents working abroad are no longer fully exempt:
- First ZAR 1,250,000 of foreign employment income per year is exempt
- Income above ZAR 1.25M is taxable in SA (with Foreign Tax Credit for taxes paid abroad)
- Must be employed abroad for ≥183 days (including ≥60 consecutive days) in a 12-month period

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
South Africa has DTAs with 80+ countries.

### DTA with India
South Africa-India DTA (1999):
- Employment income: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 10%
- Interest: WHT 10%
- Royalties: WHT 10%

## Work Visas

### Critical Skills Visa
- For occupations listed on the Critical Skills List
- 5 years; no job offer required for initial application
- One of the most flexible African work visas

### General Work Visa
- Requires employer sponsorship and Labour Market Test
- Duration: 3–5 years

### Business Visa
- For investors / company owners
- Requires minimum ZAR 5 million investment (reduced categories available)

## VAT
Standard rate: 15%
Zero-rated: basic food items, exports, international transport
Exempt: residential accommodation, financial services, education

## Cost-of-Living Context
- Cape Town 1-bedroom central: ZAR 18,000–28,000/month (~$960–$1,500 USD)
- Johannesburg: 15–20% cheaper than Cape Town for comparable areas
- Monthly MyCiTi transport (Cape Town): ZAR 600–1,000/month
- South Africa has significant infrastructure and safety challenges in some areas
- Exceptional value for international remote workers earning in hard currency
