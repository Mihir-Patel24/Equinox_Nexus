# United Kingdom — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System
The UK uses a Pay As You Earn (PAYE) system. Income tax is collected at source by employers.

### 2024/25 Tax Bands (England, Wales, Northern Ireland)
- Personal Allowance: £12,570 (0% tax)
- Basic Rate: 20% on income £12,571–£50,270
- Higher Rate: 40% on income £50,271–£125,140
- Additional Rate: 45% on income above £125,140

The Personal Allowance is reduced by £1 for every £2 earned above £100,000, reaching zero at £125,140.

### National Insurance Contributions (NIC)
Employees pay Class 1 NIC:
- 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270 per year
- 2% on earnings above £50,270

Employers pay 13.8% on earnings above £9,100 per year.

### Effective Total Tax Burden
For income £80,000: approximately 38–42% combined (income tax + NIC)
For income £50,000: approximately 30–34% combined

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
The UK has comprehensive DTAs with over 130 countries.

### DTA with India
The UK-India DTA (1993, updated 2011) prevents double taxation on:
- Salary and employment income
- Pensions and annuities
- Dividends (WHT reduced to 15%)
- Interest (WHT reduced to 15%)
- Royalties (WHT reduced to 15%)

A UK resident working in India retains UK tax residency under the tie-breaker rules if they have a permanent home in the UK, are ordinarily resident in the UK, and their centre of vital interests is in the UK.

### DTA with USA
The UK-US DTA (2001) covers employment income. US citizens in the UK still owe US taxes but receive a Foreign Tax Credit.

### Split-Year Treatment
If you arrive in or leave the UK partway through a tax year, split-year treatment may apply, reducing your UK tax liability for part of the year.

## Statutory Residence Test (SRT)
UK tax residency is determined by the SRT, not domicile:
- Present in UK 183+ days: automatically UK resident
- Present in UK fewer than 16 days: automatically non-resident
- Intermediate cases: determined by connecting factors (UK home, UK work, UK family ties)

## VAT
Standard VAT rate: 20%
Reduced rate: 5% (energy, child car seats)
Zero rate: 0% (food, books, children's clothing)

## Work Visa Categories

### Skilled Worker Visa
- Requires sponsorship from a licensed UK employer
- Job must be on the eligible occupations list (SOC codes)
- Salary threshold: £26,200/year minimum (varies by role; some roles have going rate requirements up to £40,000+)
- Points-based system: 70 points required
- English language requirement: B1 CEFR minimum

### Global Talent Visa
- For leaders and potential leaders in academia, research, digital technology, or arts
- Requires endorsement from a designated body (Tech Nation, Royal Society, etc.)
- No job offer required

### High Potential Individual (HPI) Visa
- For graduates of top global universities (Times Higher Education Top 50 within last 5 years)
- 2-year visa (3 years for PhDs)
- No sponsorship needed

## National Insurance Number (NIN)
Required to work legally. Apply online after arriving in the UK. Proof of right to work must be provided to employers.

## UK Pension — Auto-Enrolment
Employers must automatically enroll eligible workers into a workplace pension scheme if aged 22–State Pension age and earning over £10,000/year. Minimum employer contribution: 3%. Minimum employee contribution: 5%.

## Banking Considerations
Opening a UK bank account typically requires proof of address and identity. International workers should consider Monzo, Starling, or Wise as initial options before establishing UK credit history.

## Cost-of-Living Context
London is one of the most expensive cities globally. Rent for a 1-bedroom apartment in central London averages £2,200–£3,000/month (2024). Outside London, costs drop significantly (Manchester, Edinburgh, Birmingham are 30–50% cheaper).
