# Australia — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System — 2024/25 Rates (Stage 3 Tax Cuts Applied)
Australia implemented major Stage 3 tax cuts from July 1, 2024:
- Up to AUD 18,200: 0% (Tax-Free Threshold)
- AUD 18,201–45,000: 16%
- AUD 45,001–135,000: 30%
- AUD 135,001–190,000: 37%
- Above AUD 190,000: 45%

### Medicare Levy
- 2% of taxable income (surcharge for high earners without private health insurance)

### Superannuation (Super)
Mandatory employer contribution to retirement savings:
- 2024: 11.5% (increasing to 12% from July 2025)
- Employee voluntary contributions also encouraged (tax advantages)
- This is IN ADDITION to salary — effectively increases total compensation by 11.5%

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income AUD 120,000/year: approximately 33–36% (income tax + Medicare; Super is employer-paid on top)

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Australia has DTAs with 45+ countries.

### DTA with India
Australia-India DTA (1991):
- Employment income: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 15% (franked dividends may be lower)
- Interest: WHT 10%
- Royalties: WHT 10%

## Work Visas

### Temporary Skill Shortage (TSS) — subclass 482
- Employer-sponsored; requires ANZSCO skill level 1–3
- Short-term stream: 2 years (non-renewable); Limited occupations
- Medium-term stream: 4 years; leads to permanent residency
- Minimum salary: TSMIT (Temporary Skilled Migration Income Threshold) = AUD 73,150/year (2024)

### Employer Nomination Scheme — subclass 186
Permanent residency pathway from TSS 482:
- Direct entry stream for highly skilled workers
- Temporary residence transition after 2+ years on 482

### Global Talent Independent (GTI) — subclass 858
For exceptionally talented individuals in target sectors:
- AI/Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, FinTech, BioMed, Energy, AgriFood Tech, Space
- No age or language requirements
- Bypasses normal queues

### Points Test Skilled Visas (subclass 189/190/491)
- 189: Independent (no employer/state sponsorship needed)
- 190: State/territory nominated
- 491: Regional sponsored (faster processing, regional areas)

## Healthcare (Medicare)
Citizens and permanent residents access Medicare. Some countries have Reciprocal Health Care Agreements (RHCA) with Australia including UK, Italy, Belgium, Netherlands, New Zealand.

## GST
Standard rate: 10% (no reduced rates; food, healthcare, education zero-rated)
