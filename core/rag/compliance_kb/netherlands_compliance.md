Note: The 36.97% rate combines income tax + national insurance contributions (AOW, ANW, WLZ).

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income €80,000/year: approximately 43–47% combined

## 30% Ruling (Expat Tax Regime)
**The most significant expat tax benefit in Europe:**
- 30% of salary paid **tax-free** for incoming highly skilled workers
- Duration: **60 months** (5 years) from January 2024 (was 120 months until 2024)
- Eligibility:
  - Recruited from abroad (lived ≥150km from Dutch border in previous 24 months)
  - Specific salary threshold: €46,107/year (2024) — lower for under-30 with master's degree
- The effective tax rate for eligible expats: approximately 23–25% instead of 36.97–49.5%

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Netherlands has DTAs with 100+ countries (one of Europe's largest treaty networks).

### DTA with India
Netherlands-India DTA (1989):
- Salary: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 10%
- Interest: WHT 10%
- Royalties: WHT 10%

## Work Visas

### Highly Skilled Migrant (Kennismigrant) Permit
- Employer-registered with IND (Immigration and Naturalisation Service)
- Minimum salary: €5,331/month gross (2024; lower for ≤30 years: €3,909/month)
- Fast-track processing: 2 weeks
- Valid for duration of employment (max 5 years)

### EU Blue Card
- Alternative to Kennismigrant for EU Blue Card holders
- Salary threshold: €5,331/month

### Startup Visa
- For innovative entrepreneurs
- Requires a Dutch facilitator (recognized incubator)
- 1 year to develop business, then convert to self-employed permit

## Healthcare
Netherlands has a mandatory private health insurance system:
- Basic insurance: €130–180/month premium
- Statutory excess (eigen risico): €385/year
- Employer may provide supplemental coverage

## VAT (BTW)
- Standard rate: 21%
- Reduced rate: 9% (food, medicine, books, art, accommodation)
- Zero rate: 0% (exports, international transport)

## Cost-of-Living Context
- Amsterdam 1-bedroom central: €1,800–€2,800/month (extremely tight rental market)
- Rotterdam/The Hague: 20–30% cheaper than Amsterdam
- Monthly OV-chipkaart (public transit): approximately €80–120/month depending on commute
- Cycling infrastructure is world-class — many residents skip transit costs entirely
