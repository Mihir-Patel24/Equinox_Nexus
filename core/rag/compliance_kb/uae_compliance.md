# United Arab Emirates (Dubai/Abu Dhabi) — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax
The UAE has **zero personal income tax**. This applies to all residents regardless of nationality. There is no:
- Personal income tax
- Capital gains tax (on personal investments)
- Inheritance/estate tax
- Wealth tax

This makes the UAE one of the most tax-efficient jurisdictions globally for high-income professionals.

## Corporate Tax
As of June 1, 2023, a federal Corporate Tax of 9% applies to businesses with net profits exceeding AED 375,000/year.
- Free zone businesses may qualify for 0% corporate tax on qualifying income under specific conditions
- Small businesses earning <AED 375,000 net profit: 0% CT
- Qualifying Public Benefit Entities are exempt

## Value Added Tax (VAT)
VAT rate: 5% (one of the lowest in the world, introduced January 2018)
Zero-rated: healthcare, education, residential real estate (first supply), exports
Exempt: local passenger transport, bare land

## Social Security / Pension
- **UAE nationals**: Must contribute to GPSSA. Employee: 5%, Employer: 12.5%
- **Expatriates**: Not subject to UAE social security contributions

## Free Zones
Dubai has over 40 free zones (DIFC, JAFZA, DMCC, DIC, DMC, Dubai South, etc.):
- 100% foreign ownership
- 0% corporate tax on qualifying income
- 100% repatriation of profits
- Exemption from import/export duties within the free zone

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
UAE has signed DTAs with 140+ countries (one of the world's largest treaty networks).

### DTA with India
The UAE-India DTAA (1993, revised 2016) is exceptionally beneficial:
- Salary income earned in UAE: NOT taxable in India if you qualify as UAE resident (183+ days/year)
- Dividends: 10% WHT maximum
- Interest: 12.5% WHT maximum
- Royalties: 10% WHT maximum

### DTA with USA
US citizens are still subject to US worldwide taxation regardless of UAE residence.
FEIE ($126,500 for 2024) can offset UAE employment income.

## Visa Categories

### Employment Visa
- Sponsored by employer
- Valid 2–3 years, renewable
- Worker's rights protected under UAE Labour Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021)

### Golden Visa (Long-Term Residence — 10 Years)
For:
- Real estate investors (≥AED 2M property)
- Business investors (≥AED 2M deposit)
- Entrepreneurs (approved/registered project)
- Exceptional talents (sports, arts, science, medicine, etc.)
- Graduates of top 200 global universities
- Doctors, scientists, innovators in key fields

### Green Visa (5-Year Self-Sponsorship)
For skilled workers and freelancers:
- Requires a degree and salary ≥AED 15,000/month, OR
- Freelance permit with minimum income AED 360,000/year

## End of Service Gratuity (ESG)
After 1 year of service, employer must pay:
- 21 days of base salary per year of service (first 5 years)
- 30 days per year thereafter
This is legally mandated and tax-free. Capped at 2 years of base salary.

## Cost-of-Living Context
- Dubai 1-bedroom in Downtown/Marina: AED 8,000–14,000/month (~$2,200–$3,800 USD)
- Abu Dhabi: 20–30% cheaper than Dubai for comparable areas
- Sharjah, Ajman: 40–50% cheaper; many residents commute to Dubai
- Utility costs are high (AED 600–1,200/month typical)
- Healthcare: private insurance mandatory; employer usually provides
