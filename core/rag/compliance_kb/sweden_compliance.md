# Sweden — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System
Sweden has one of the highest income tax rates globally, but also one of the most comprehensive welfare states.

### Municipal Tax
- Average municipal (kommunal) tax: **30–33%** of taxable income (varies by municipality)
- Stockholm: approximately 29.8%
- Gothenburg: approximately 32.35%

### State Tax (Statlig Skatt)
- 20% on income above SEK 598,500/year (~€53,000)

### Social Security Contributions
- Employee: 7% (general pension contribution)
- Employer: 31.42% — one of the highest in the world

### Effective Total Tax Burden
- Income SEK 700,000/year: approximately 50–56% combined
- The high taxes fund free healthcare, free education (including university), and generous parental leave

## Researcher/Expert Tax Relief (Expertskatten)
- 25% of income exempt from Swedish income tax for 3 years (extendable to 5)
- Available to: foreign experts, researchers, executives earning above SEK 109,302/month
- Eligibility: must not have been resident in Sweden in the preceding 5 years

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Sweden has DTAs with over 100 countries.

### DTA with India
Sweden-India DTA (1997):
- Employment income: taxed where performed
- Dividends: WHT 10%
- Interest: WHT 10%
- Royalties: WHT 10%

## Work Visas

### EU Blue Card
- For highly skilled non-EU workers
- Minimum salary: 1.5× average (≈SEK 46,500/month)
- Valid 2 years, renewable

### Work Permit (Arbetstillstånd)
- Employer-sponsored; employer must advertise position in Sweden/EU first
- Approved by the Swedish Migration Agency (Migrationsverket)
- Valid for duration of employment, up to 2 years; renewable

## Social Benefits
- Universal healthcare (1177 system)
- Dental: covered up to age 23, then subsidized
- Parental leave: 480 days per child, paid at ~80% of salary
- Unemployment benefit: 80% of previous salary for 300 days

## VAT (Moms)
- Standard rate: 25%
- Reduced rate: 12% (food, hotels, restaurant services)
- Reduced rate: 6% (books, newspapers, public transport)

## Cost-of-Living Context
- Stockholm 1-bedroom central: SEK 15,000–22,000/month (~€1,350–€2,000)
- Gothenburg: 15–20% cheaper than Stockholm
- Monthly SL transport pass (Stockholm): SEK 930/month (~€85)
- High quality of life, excellent public services, but significant taxes
