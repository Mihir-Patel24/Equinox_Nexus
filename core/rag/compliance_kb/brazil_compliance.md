# Brazil — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax (IRPF) — 2024 Rates
Brazil updated its income tax rates in 2024:
- Up to BRL 2,824/month (BRL 33,888/year): 0%
- BRL 2,825–3,751/month: 7.5%
- BRL 3,752–4,664/month: 15%
- BRL 4,665–5,563/month: 22.5%
- Above BRL 5,563/month: 27.5%

### Social Contributions (Employee)
- INSS (Social Security): 7.5–14% (progressive on gross salary)
- FGTS (Severance Fund, paid by employer): 8% of gross salary

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Brazil has a high cumulative tax burden when including indirect taxes.
Income BRL 120,000/year: approximately 28–35% direct taxes

## Foreign Resident Tax Rules
Foreigners with tax residency in Brazil are taxed on worldwide income.
Brazilian non-residents are taxed at a flat 25% on Brazilian-sourced income.

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Brazil has relatively few DTAs (approximately 34 countries). It does NOT have a DTA with the USA or UK.
Countries with Brazil DTAs: China, India, Portugal, Japan, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, and others.

### DTA with India
Brazil-India DTA (1992):
- Employment income: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 15%
- Interest: WHT 15%
- Royalties: WHT 15–25%

## Work Visas

### VITEM V (Temporary Work Visa)
- Requires employment contract with Brazilian entity
- Authorized by Ministry of Labour and Employment (MTE)
- Duration: up to 2 years (renewable)

### Tech Nomad Visa (2024 development)
Brazil is implementing framework for digital nomads in 2024.

## VAT Equivalent (Complex System)
Brazil's tax system is notoriously complex with multiple taxes:
- ICMS (state): 12–25% (most goods and services)
- ISS (municipal): 2–5% (services)
- PIS/COFINS: 3.65% or 9.25% (on revenue)
- IPI (federal manufacturing): varies by product

**2023–2024 Tax Reform**: Major reform simplifying VAT into CBS (federal) and IBS (state/municipal) — implementation phased through 2033.

## Cost-of-Living Context
- Sao Paulo 1-bedroom central: BRL 4,000–7,000/month (~$700–$1,300 USD)
- Rio de Janeiro: similar to Sao Paulo
- Brasilia, Belo Horizonte: 15–25% cheaper
- Brazil is a large country; costs vary enormously by region
- Strong tech and startup ecosystem in Sao Paulo (Faria Lima district = Brazilian Silicon Valley)
