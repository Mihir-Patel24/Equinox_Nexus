# Mexico — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax (ISR — Impuesto Sobre la Renta) — 2024 Rates
Mexico has a progressive income tax system:
- Up to MXN 8,952/year: 1.92%
- MXN 8,953–75,984: 6.4%
- MXN 75,985–133,536: 10.88%
- MXN 133,537–155,232: 16%
- MXN 155,233–185,852: 17.92%
- MXN 185,853–374,838: 21.36%
- MXN 374,839–590,796: 23.52%
- MXN 590,797–1,127,926: 30%
- MXN 1,127,927–1,503,902: 32%
- Above MXN 1,503,903: 35%

### Social Security (IMSS) — Employee Contributions
Approximately 2.4–3% of salary plus fixed amounts by category.
Employer pays much larger portion: approximately 20–25% of payroll.

### Effective Total Tax Burden
Income MXN 600,000/year: approximately 26–32% combined

## Digital Nomad Visa (Temporary Resident for Remote Workers)
Mexico does not have a specific digital nomad visa but Temporary Resident visa is commonly used:
- Proof of sufficient income (≈MXN 35,000/month or savings equivalent)
- Initial 1-year (renewable up to 4 years)
- No formal work authorization for remote work for foreign companies

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Mexico has DTAs with 70+ countries.

### DTA with India
Mexico-India DTA (2010):
- Employment income: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 10%
- Interest: WHT 10%
- Royalties: WHT 10%

## VAT (IVA)
- Standard rate: 16%
- Border zone rate: 8% (for areas within 20km of US/Guatemala/Belize border)
- Zero rate: 0% (food, medicine)

## Cost-of-Living Context
Mexico City is one of the best value major cities globally:
- Mexico City 1-bedroom central (Polanco/Roma): MXN 18,000–35,000/month (~$1,050–$2,050 USD)
- Guadalajara, Monterrey: 20–30% cheaper than CDMX for comparable areas
- Monthly Metro pass (CDMX): approximately MXN 150/month (~$9)
- Strong food culture; extremely affordable eating
- Growing tech scene: Mexico City is a major startup hub for Latin America
