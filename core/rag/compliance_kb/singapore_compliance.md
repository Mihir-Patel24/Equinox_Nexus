# Singapore — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System — 2024 Rates
Singapore has one of the most competitive personal tax regimes globally:
- Up to SGD 20,000: 0%
- SGD 20,001–30,000: 2%
- SGD 30,001–40,000: 3.5%
- SGD 40,001–80,000: 7%
- SGD 80,001–120,000: 11.5%
- SGD 120,001–160,000: 15%
- SGD 160,001–200,000: 18%
- SGD 200,001–240,000: 19%
- SGD 240,001–280,000: 19.5%
- SGD 280,001–320,000: 20%
- Above SGD 320,000: 22%

**No capital gains tax. No inheritance tax. No dividends tax.**

### Central Provident Fund (CPF)
CPF is a mandatory savings scheme for Singapore citizens and PRs only (not applicable to foreigners on Employment Pass):
- Employee: 20% (varies by age)
- Employer: 17% (varies by age)
EP holders are NOT required to contribute to CPF.

### Effective Total Tax Burden (Employment Pass Holder)
Income SGD 150,000/year: approximately 15–17%
This is one of the lowest effective tax rates for high earners globally.

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Singapore has DTAs with 90+ countries (one of the most extensive networks).

### DTA with India
Singapore-India DTA (1994, updated 2016):
- Capital gains: Singapore has the taxing right on gains from Indian investments IF not structured to abuse
- Dividends: WHT 10–15%
- Interest: WHT 10%
- Royalties: WHT 10%
IMPORTANT: The 2016 amendment limited treaty benefits for capital gains — investors should obtain tax advice.

## Work Visas

### Employment Pass (EP)
- For foreign professionals, managers, executives
- Minimum salary: SGD 5,600/month (2024); SGD 6,200 for financial services sector
- Required: degree-level qualification or recognized professional qualifications
- Initial 2 years, renewable for 3 years

### S Pass (Mid-Level Skills)
- For mid-level skilled workers
- Minimum salary: SGD 3,150/month
- Employer quota: 15% of total workforce (10% for services sector)

### EntrePass
- For entrepreneurs wishing to start a business in Singapore
- Must have venture-backed funding or hold an Intellectual Property

### Tech.Pass
- For established tech entrepreneurs and leaders
- Minimum salary/revenue thresholds apply
- 2-year renewable, no employer sponsorship required

## GST (Goods and Services Tax)
Standard rate: 9% (increased from 8% in 2024)

## Cost-of-Living Context
Singapore is one of Asia's most expensive cities:
- 1-bedroom condo central: SGD 3,500–5,500/month ($2,600–$4,100 USD)
- Public housing (HDB flats): only available to PRs/citizens; foreigner market is private condos
- Monthly MRT/bus pass: SGD 128/month (~$95)
- No car culture recommended — Cars are extremely expensive (COE premiums add SGD 80,000+ to car price)
- World-class healthcare — government polyclinics subsidized; private hospitals are premium-priced
