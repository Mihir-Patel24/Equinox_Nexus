# Thailand — Tax, Compliance & Visa Intelligence

## Income Tax System — 2024 Rates
Thailand has a progressive income tax system for residents:
- Up to THB 150,000: 0% (exempt)
- THB 150,001–300,000: 5%
- THB 300,001–500,000: 10%
- THB 500,001–750,000: 15%
- THB 750,001–1,000,000: 20%
- THB 1,000,001–2,000,000: 25%
- THB 2,000,001–5,000,000: 30%
- Above THB 5,000,000: 35%

### Tax Residency Rule (2024 Update)
From 2024, Thailand changed its remittance rule:
- **Old rule**: Only foreign income remitted to Thailand in the same calendar year was taxable
- **New rule (from 2024)**: Foreign income earned from January 1, 2024 onward is taxable upon remittance regardless of when earned
This significantly affects digital nomads and retirees living in Thailand.

### Social Security
- Employee: 5% (capped at THB 750/month on first THB 15,000 salary)
- Employer: 5%
Foreign employees on Thai payroll are covered by Thai social security.

## Double Taxation Agreements (DTA)
Thailand has DTAs with 61 countries.

### DTA with India
Thailand-India DTA (1986):
- Salary: taxed where work performed
- Dividends: WHT 20% (reduced to 10% if beneficial owner holds ≥25%)
- Interest: WHT 25% (reduced to 10% if recipient is government/financial institution)

## Work Visas / Permits

### Non-Immigrant B Visa + Work Permit
- Requires sponsoring employer
- Work permit issued by Department of Employment
- Minimum Thai employees: 4 Thais per 1 foreign employee

### Long-Term Resident (LTR) Visa
Introduced in 2022; 10-year renewable visa for:
- Wealthy global citizens: passive income ≥$80,000/year + $1M+ assets
- Wealthy pensioners: income ≥$40,000/year
- Work-from-Thailand professionals: income ≥$80,000/year from overseas employer
- Highly skilled professionals in targeted industries
Benefits: 17% flat income tax rate for work-from-Thailand category (vs 35% standard)

### Thailand Elite Visa (PRIVILEGE Card)
- 5 or 20-year membership program
- Costs: THB 600,000–2,000,000
- No work authorization; for tourism/retirement purposes

## VAT
Standard rate: 7% (reduced from 10% — extended annually)

## Cost-of-Living Context
- Bangkok 1-bedroom central: THB 15,000–35,000/month (~$430–$1,000)
- Chiang Mai: 30–40% cheaper than Bangkok
- Excellent healthcare (Bumrungrad, Bangkok Hospital) at a fraction of Western costs
- Strong expat and digital nomad community in Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket
